Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Awakening Minds is a free guided meditation app, published by Edouard Richemond in France. It requires no account, shows no ads and offers no purchases. This policy explains in plain terms what happens to your information when you use it.

1. In short

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your journal, notes, written meditations, favorites, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We use no audience measurement, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell any data.
  • The app connects to the internet only to download meditation audio and its updates.

2. What stays on your device

What you create or choose in the app is stored only on your phone: journal entries, notes, texts in "My meditations", favorites, history, points, and your language, voice, speed, pause and reminder preferences. We have no access to it. Everything is deleted when you uninstall the app. If your device backup (iCloud or Google) is turned on, this data may be included in it, depending on your own settings.

3. Audio downloads

The audio for a meditation is downloaded from our file server, hosted by Cloudflare (audio.awakeningminds.app), the first time you listen to it. It is then kept on your device for offline playback. As with any website, each file request sends technical information: IP address, device type and time. Cloudflare uses this information to deliver the files and protect the service. We do not use it to identify you or to build a profile.

4. App updates

When it starts, the app checks whether an update to its content or interface is available, using the EAS Update service from Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States). This check sends the platform, the app version and a random identifier created at installation. This identifier contains no personal information, is not linked to your identity or to other apps, and is used only to distribute updates. It disappears when you uninstall the app.

5. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled directly on your device, with no notification server. Permission to send notifications is requested only if you turn on a reminder, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone settings.

6. Reading voices

The meditations are read by synthetic voices recorded in advance. No text or data from you is sent to a speech synthesis service. Texts in "My meditations" are read by the voices built into your phone, without a connection.

7. Permissions

The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location or photos. The only permission requested is for notifications, when you turn on a reminder.

8. Sharing

When you share the app, a meditation or an audio diagnostic report, your phone's share sheet opens. You choose the recipient, and we receive nothing.

9. App stores

Apple and Google, which distribute the app, may provide us with overall, anonymous statistics, such as the number of downloads or crashes, according to their own policies and your settings. These statistics do not allow us to identify you.

10. Legal basis and retention

We do not keep any file of personal data. The technical processing described in sections 3 and 4 is based on our legitimate interest in delivering the app and its content securely. Cloudflare and Expo keep their technical logs according to their own policies.

11. Transfers outside the European Union

Cloudflare and Expo may process this technical data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

12. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect any information about children.

13. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, for example the GDPR in the European Union, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection. Since we hold no data that could identify you, the simplest way to erase everything is to uninstall the app. If you have any questions, write to us. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country; in France, this is the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

14. Changes

If our practices change, this policy will be updated here and in the app, with a new date.
