Guided meditation: the archives, reunions and what lies beyond

3 sessions24 to 25 minintermediate

Higher Knowledge & Beyond contains three longer journeys. In The Keeper of the Archives, you learn to tell what the mind invents from what seems to arise on its own. The Garden of Reunion lets you say the words left unsaid and feel what passes from one generation to the next. My Departed Animal helps you reconnect with an animal who has passed, through everyday memories.

Each session lasts 24 to 25 minutes and is marked as intermediate. They are best enjoyed when you have time and a quiet space, and they suit anyone drawn to memory, family ties and inner exploration. Emotions may come up along the way, so give yourself a calm moment afterwards before returning to your day.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Akashic records?

The Akashic records are described as a kind of memory said to hold every experience. In The Keeper of the Archives, the focus stays practical: noticing what your mind builds and what seems to appear by itself, without asking you to adopt any belief.

Is My Departed Animal suitable after a recent loss?

The session works gently with everyday memories of your animal. Emotions may come up, and that is natural. Listen when you feel ready, and feel free to pause whenever you need to.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for around half an hour. The sessions last 24 to 25 minutes, and it is worth taking a quiet moment afterwards, perhaps to write a few notes, before returning to the rest of your day.
