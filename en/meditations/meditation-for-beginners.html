Guided meditation for beginners

8 sessions5 to 19 minbeginner

This category is where to start if you have never meditated, or if you want to rebuild your practice on solid ground. Eight short sessions cover the essentials one step at a time: a body scan, steadying attention on the breath, telling sensations, emotions and thoughts apart, watching thoughts go by, mindful eating, opening the five senses and meditating with your eyes open. The last one is your first silent meditation.

Sessions last between 5 and 19 minutes and are all designed for beginners. You can follow them in order or pick the theme that speaks to you today. Like everything in Awakening Minds, they are free, need no account and play offline once downloaded.

The sessions

  1. Body Scan — Mapping the Body from Within

    17 min · beginner

    Travel through the body like a still-imperfect map, region by region, learning to feel before changing anything.

  2. Stabilizing Attention on the Breath — Ariadne’s Thread

    13 min · beginner

    Make the breath a meeting point: return to it each time attention drifts, without breathing perfectly.

  3. Distinguishing Sensation, Emotion, and Thought — the Three Layers of Experience

    14 min · beginner

    Learn to tell apart what the body feels, what emotion colours, and what thought narrates.

  4. Meditating with Your Eyes Open — Staying Present in the Midst of the World

    12 min · beginner

    Meditate with open eyes and a relaxed gaze, to stay present in the middle of a room, a street, the world.

  5. Observing Thoughts — Watching the Trains Go by

    15 min · beginner

    Watch thoughts go by like trains: see them arrive, name them, let them leave.

  6. Mindful Eating — Rediscovering a Single Bite

    13 min · beginner

    A single bite, taken slowly: texture, smell, taste, until an everyday gesture feels new again.

  7. Awareness of the Five Senses — Opening the Five Doors

    19 min · beginner

    Open the five windows of awareness one by one — sight, hearing, touch, smell, taste — then leave them all open.

  8. Your First Silent Meditation

    5 min · beginner

    A first session with real silences: they are not emptiness, they are the practice itself.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a beginner meditate?

There is no fixed rule. The sessions in this category last from 5 to 19 minutes, which is a comfortable range when you are starting out. A short session done regularly is usually easier to keep up than a long one you postpone.

Do I need any experience or equipment?

No. Each session explains what to do as you go, and all you need is a quiet place to sit or lie down and your phone. Headphones can help you stay focused, but they are not required.

In what order should I follow the sessions?

You can follow them in the order they appear, starting with the body scan and the breath, then moving on to thoughts and the senses. Your first silent meditation makes a natural final step once the other sessions feel familiar.
