Learn to meditate… or simply escape

The meditations draw on techniques from some of the world's leading meditation experts, with a real goal: helping you grow. Illustrated lessons, libraries and plenty of exercises across many subjects, for every level — start from scratch, or build on an intermediate practice all the way to expert. And if you're not looking to learn anything at all, this app is made for you too: drift through wondrous worlds and savour moments of beauty and peace. 100% free, no ads, no account, no data collected.

  • - Download once, meditate anywhere: plane, mountain, subway

Frequently asked questions

Is the app really 100% free?

Yes, 100% free: no paid meditations, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium tier. Meditation should be accessible to everyone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No sign-up, no email. You install the app, pick your language and voice, and start your first guided meditation. Your data stays on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It's a free meditation app, offline too: every meditation and world downloads once, then plays anywhere — on a plane, in the mountains, on the subway. Only the first play needs a connection. That includes long guided journeys of up to 41 minutes and the sleep sounds, so the app is ready for flights, night trains and places with no signal.

How do I use MeditaDream to learn how to meditate?

Start with the Learning Basics category: posture, breath and attention, step by step, best done in order. Pick a voice and a slower pace while you are new, then jot down how you feel in a journal. Your zen passport keeps track of your progress. Learn to meditate with MeditaDream →

How can MeditaDream help me fall asleep?

At bedtime, play a sleep meditation from Body, Calm & Sleep: some of them end without a wake-up so you can drift off while listening. You can also play an ambient sound on its own, with a timer that fades it out after 5 to 60 minutes. Sleep better with MeditaDream →

How do I use MeditaDream for stress relief?

When tension builds, a session from the 4 Minutes category is enough to breathe and release your body, even at work. Not sure which one to pick? Tell the Oracle how you feel and it suggests three meditations that fit your mood right now. Stress relief with MeditaDream →

How is it different from other meditation apps?

Everything is free — no subscription, no purchases; some meditations unlock with points earned by practising, never bought — and sessions play offline once downloaded: 144 guided meditations and 17 immersive worlds, three languages, two voices each.

Are there meditations for sleep?

Yes — a whole Body, Calm & Sleep category, with guided sleep meditations, deep relaxations and a dream journal for the morning after. → Read: Guided sleep meditation

What techniques are the meditations based on?

On techniques used by some of the world's leading meditation experts and teachers: body scan, breath anchoring, noting thoughts, visualization, loving-kindness… The goal is real learning, with teaching sections, libraries and plenty of exercises across many subjects.

Is it for beginners or experienced meditators?

Both. Exercises range from the very first steps to advanced practice, and every meditation shows its level. Start from scratch with Learning Basics (posture, breathing, attention, in short sessions), or build on an intermediate practice all the way to expert. → Read: How to start meditating

What if I don't want to learn anything?

Then this zen meditation app is made for you too. Simply let go and explore wondrous worlds like the Floating Island, Underwater Atlantis or the Dream Forest, and enjoy moments of beauty and peace — no exercises, no goals.
