Frequently asked questions

Is the app really 100% free?

Yes, 100% free: no paid meditations, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium tier. Meditation should be accessible to everyone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. No sign-up, no email. You install the app, pick your language and voice, and start your first guided meditation. Your data stays on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Yes: every meditation and world downloads once, then plays anywhere — on a plane, in the mountains, on the subway. Only the first play needs a connection. That includes long guided journeys of up to 41 minutes and the sleep sounds, so the app is ready for flights, night trains and places with no signal.

How is it different from other meditation apps?

Everything is free — no subscription, no purchases; some meditations unlock with points earned by practising, never bought — and sessions play offline once downloaded: 129 guided meditations and 17 immersive worlds, three languages, two voices each.

Are there meditations for sleep?

Yes — a whole Body, Calm & Sleep category, with guided sleep meditations, deep relaxations and a dream journal for the morning after. → Read: Guided sleep meditation

What techniques are the meditations based on?

On techniques used by some of the world's leading meditation experts and teachers: body scan, breath anchoring, noting thoughts, visualization, loving-kindness… The goal is real learning, with teaching sections, libraries and plenty of exercises across many subjects.

Is it for beginners or experienced meditators?

Both. Exercises range from the very first steps to advanced practice, and every meditation shows its level. Start from scratch with Learning Basics (posture, breathing, attention, in short sessions), or build on an intermediate practice all the way to expert. → Read: How to start meditating

What if I don't want to learn anything?

Then this app is made for you too. Simply let go and explore wondrous worlds like the Floating Island, Underwater Atlantis or the Dream Forest, and enjoy moments of beauty and peace — no exercises, no goals.
