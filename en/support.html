Frequently asked

Is MeditaDream really free?

Yes — 100 % free, no ads, no subscription, no accounts required. Half of the meditations and all worlds are open from install; the rest unlock with points earned by practising, never bought.

How do I download meditations for offline?

Open any meditation and tap “Download for offline”, or play it once with a connection: the audio is stored on your device, then plays without network.

How do I delete my data?

Simply uninstall the app — all data is stored locally on your device. No account to close.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sessions are for relaxation, inner exploration and well-being. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and do not replace the support of a qualified professional.
