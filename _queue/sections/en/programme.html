Beginner program — 4 weeks

From the very first breath to autonomy

A marked, progressive and realistic path: a few minutes a day is enough. Each week introduces a new brick and consolidates the previous one. If you miss a day, simply resume the next — regularity rebuilds itself, it doesn't get lost.

WeekGoalDaily practice
Week 1
Lay the foundations		 Posture + abdominal breathing 5 min/day — Set up posture (2 min), then hand on belly: 10 abdominal breaths, and free observation of the breath. Days 6–7: try the interactive guide in coherence 5-5.
Week 2
Anchor attention		 Mindful breathing + attention cycle 8 min/day — Mindful breathing with counting 1 to 10. Train “noticing and returning”: each return counts. Every other day, finish with 2 min of sounds.
Week 3
Explore the body		 Body scan + walking 10 min/day — Alternate: body scan (odd days), walking meditation (even days). Add a daily anchor: 3 conscious breaths at a chosen trigger-gesture.
Week 4
Open up		 Mindfulness + discovery 12–15 min/day — Full mindfulness session (breath → sounds → sensations → open presence). Try 2 new techniques from the menu (Metta, mantra, visualization…) to find your affinities.
💡 After the 4 weeksYou're autonomous: keep a daily base of 10–15 minutes with your favorite technique, and dip into the menu according to the day's needs (cross-reference table above). Next goal: a 20-minute session, once a week.
