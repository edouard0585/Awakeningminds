Frequently asked

Is Awakening Minds really free?

Yes — 100 % free, no ads, no subscription, no accounts required. Every meditation and world is unlocked over time as you practice.

How do I download meditations for offline?

Open any meditation, then tap "Download offline" — every audio segment is cached on your device so you can play without network.

How do I delete my data?

Simply uninstall the app — all data is stored locally on your device. No account to close.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sessions are for relaxation, inner exploration and well-being. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and do not replace the support of a qualified professional.
